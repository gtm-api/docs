---
title: "Find an email or phone number for a profile"
description: "Run contact enrichment on one LinkedIn profile: what comes back, when it runs free on your own connected account versus the managed pool, what each path costs in credits, and how to read a partial result."
---

Enrichment pulls one known person's own data from LinkedIn through a live browser
session. This page covers the two methods that can return an email or a phone number,
where each one can run, and how to read the receipt that comes back. A list of other
people (search results, employees, post reactions) is scraping instead: different surface,
different pricing.

## Before you start

- A connected LinkedIn account for the free path, or
  [credits](/kb/credits-and-data-requests) on the team balance for the managed path.
- The `can_act_linkedin_enrichment` permission on your API key, agent or user.
- One identifier: `profile_id` (`ACoAA...` or `ACwAA...`) **or** `public_identifier` (the
  vanity slug from `/in/...`). Exactly one, never both, or the call is
  `422 validation_failed`.

## The two methods that return contact details

| | `person-contact-info` | `person-full-profile` |
|---|---|---|
| Contact fields | `email`, `phones[]` (typed), `websites[]`, `twitter_handles[]` | flat `email`, `phone`, `twitter`, `facebook` |
| Everything else | nothing else | headline, position, company, about, experience, and the rest |
| List price | 5 credits | 5 credits |
| What you are actually charged | always `0` | 5 on the managed path, 0 on your own account or a cache hit |
| Cache TTL | 24 hours | 24 hours |
| Where it can run | only an account of yours that is a 1st-degree connection | your pinned account, or our managed pool |

Both ride the same read but cache under their own key: a cached full profile does not
satisfy a contact-info call, and the reverse is also true.

<Warning>
The flat `phone` on `person-full-profile` keeps only the first mobile number. Home and
work numbers are dropped. `person-contact-info` returns `phones[]` with a `type` on each
entry (`MOBILE`, `HOME`, `WORK`, or `null`), so use it when the phone actually matters.
</Warning>

## Run it

```bash curl
curl -X POST "https://app.gtm-api.com/linkedin/v4/api/linkedin-enrichment/person-contact-info" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer gtm_live_YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"public_identifier": "williamhgates", "idempotency_key": "contact-whg-2026-08-13"}'
```

No executor fields there: contact info is routed to whichever of your accounts is
connected to the person, and is never charged. The call is synchronous, and the data
comes back under `result.contact_info`, next to a `credits` block and the ledger row at
`result.data_request` (`sid` starting `er_rq_`, `status`, `charged`, `executed_on`,
`served_from_cache`, `cached_from_sid`). `credits.balance_after` is `null` on every free
path (own-account run, contact info, cache hit) by design, so read the balance from
`POST https://app.gtm-api.com/id/v4/api/credit-transactions/get-balance` with body
`{"kind": "enrichment"}` instead. Per-method shapes are in the
[API reference](/api-reference/overview), and batching is your own loop (no mass action,
no sequence step), one target per call.

`contact_info` is always present, and every field inside it can be empty. An empty block
means the connected account still cannot see those details, never that the call was
blocked. `address`, `birthday` and connection date are not parsed. A phone with
`type: null` is normal on older cached payloads, not a defect.

## Your own account versus the managed pool

Every enrichment method except contact info takes exactly one executor field.

| What you send | What happens |
|---|---|
| `linkedin_account_sid` (`ln_ac_` + 12 chars) | Runs on that account only, spending that account's `enrichment` daily budget. `charged: 0`, `executed_on: "own_account"` |
| `use_credits: true` | Runs on our managed infrastructure. Debits the method price, `executed_on: "infra_pool"`, `reason: "infra_pool"` |
| Neither | `422 validation_failed`, with `field_errors.use_credits` starting `executor_required:`. Nothing runs, nothing is ledgered |
| Both | `422 validation_failed`. A pinned executor never pools, so the pair is contradictory |
| `idempotency_key` (max 128 chars) | Replay guard scoped to (team, key). A repeat returns the stored row: no re-execution, no second charge |

<Warning>
There is no silent fallback. If the account you pinned is out of daily budget the call
returns `429 rate_limited`, reason `bucket_saturated`, with a `retry_after` timestamp. It
will not quietly move to the paid path and spend credits you did not opt into.
</Warning>

### Contact info is the exception

`person-contact-info` requires a connected executor: LinkedIn reveals contact details to
1st-degree connections only, and a pool account would come back with an empty block.

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| One of your accounts holds the 1st-degree edge and has budget left | Runs there, `charged: 0` |
| Your accounts hold the edge but all of them are out of budget | `429 rate_limited`, reason `bucket_saturated`, with `retry_after` and a `connected_accounts` count |
| Nobody on the team is connected to this person | `422 no_connected_account`: "none of your LinkedIn accounts is connected to this person, so this request cannot be run." Send a connection request, retry once it is accepted |
| You passed `use_credits: true` | `422 validation_failed`: this method never runs on the credit pool, so omit the field |
| The target has neither a member id nor a slug to match on | `422 not_dispatchable` |

Here `linkedin_account_sid` is a preference, not a command: another connected account is
picked if the one you named does not qualify, and accounts out of budget, on hold or
LinkedIn rate-limited are dropped before the pick. The 5 credit list price shown on
**Person contact info** describes the underlying read; this route never bills it.

## What each path costs

| Method | Credits (list) | Cache TTL |
|---|---|---|
| `person-lite-profile`, `person-basic-profile` | 1 | 24 hours |
| The rest (experience, skills, education, posts, certifications, company reads) | 1 to 3 | 7 days |
| `company-public-identifier` | 1 | 30 days |

Credit cost is a list price, not a bill: `charged` is `0` on own-account runs, cache hits,
failures and refusals. A repeat for the same person and method inside the TTL is served
from the stored result (`served_from_cache: true`, `charged: 0`, nothing dispatched); past
it, the call executes and charges again. Cache is per team, per method.

<Warning>
When the balance is below the method price the managed path returns
`402 insufficient_credits` with `{required, available, data_request_sid}`, the row is
ledgered `rejected`, and nothing runs: no queue, no pause. Completed rows do replay free
on the same `idempotency_key`, but the rejected tail does not resume that way: the stored
refusal is returned and re-throws the same 402. After topping up, re-issue the failed
targets with new `idempotency_key` values.
</Warning>

## How to read a partial result

An empty answer is a real answer. A person who never published a phone number produces a
`completed` row with an empty field, not an error.

| What the receipt shows | What happened |
|---|---|
| `served_from_cache: true`, `charged: 0`, `cached_from_sid: "er_rq_..."` | Served from your team's cached copy. Open `GET /api/data-requests/{cached_from_sid}` and read its `completed_at` for the true age of the data |
| `served_from_cache: false` | Ran live: `executed_on: "own_account"` spent your account's daily budget and is `charged: 0`, `executed_on: "infra_pool"` ran on managed infrastructure and `charged` the method price |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `running` | Dispatched to a browser, visible mid-flight |
| `completed` | Result stored, whether executed or served from cache |
| `rejected` | Refused before dispatch, always `charged: 0` |
| `failed` | Dispatched and terminally failed, hold released, `charged: 0` |

A row is inserted either `running` or already terminal; `running` then moves to
`completed`, `failed` or `rejected` (a credit reserve failure rejects an already running
row). Nonzero `charged` on a `failed` row is a billing defect: send us the `sid`.

A call has a hard deadline of 90 seconds; typical latency is 30 seconds at p95 executed,
500 ms at p95 cache-served. Retrying is safe when you reuse the `idempotency_key`; inside
that window the replay returns the `running` row, so poll `GET /api/data-requests/{sid}`
until the status is terminal. To follow results without polling, subscribe to the
`data-requests.completed` and `data-requests.failed` webhooks; the completed payload adds
`result_ref`, `charge_reason` and `completed_at` to the receipt fields. Refusals have no
event: a rejection appears only as the synchronous HTTP error.

## Daily budget on the free path

Own-account enrichment spends one shared bucket called `enrichment`. Read it before a
batch, on the Smart Limits tab of the account drawer or through
[smart limits and warmup](/kb/smart-limits-and-warmup) filtered on `limit_type`.

<Warning>
Default budget: 80 enrichment actions per day per account, with a 240 second gap enforced
between counted actions. When the budget is spent or the gap has not elapsed the call
returns `429 rate_limited`, reason `bucket_saturated`, with a `retry_after` value; retry
after that timestamp, not on a fixed schedule. The free plan clamps this to 12 enrichments
per day per connected account.
</Warning>

## Errors you will actually hit

| Scenario | HTTP | Code | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slug-only call on a method that needs a stored profile URN | 422 | `not_dispatchable` | Run `person-lite-profile` for that slug first: it stores the URN, and the sub-record methods work from then on |
| No managed capacity, or a transient browser or plugin failure | 503 | `infrastructure_unavailable`, `plugin_unavailable` | Back off and retry the same call; `plugin_unavailable` carries a `retry_after` one minute out |
| Terminal execution failure | 409 | `operation_blocked` | Do not retry as sent, change the input |
| Method not implemented yet (`person-languages`, `post-details`) | 501 | `not_implemented` | Nothing is ledgered: no row, no credit, no budget spent |

A 429 while your credit balance is healthy is not a billing problem: credits and account
budgets are separate gates, and `bucket_saturated` is your account's daily enrichment
budget. To pay for that call instead, re-issue with `use_credits: true` and no
`linkedin_account_sid`.
